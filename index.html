Genetics set the ceiling — bone structure, canthal tilt, and jaw width are mostly fixed and no exercise or product changes them. These techniques work on what's actually changeable day to day: body fat, posture, grooming, skin, hair, and how you move and speak. That's most of what people are really reacting to when they call someone sharp, put-together, or magnetic. Don't turn this into chasing someone else's idea of a "perfect face" — use it to improve grooming, health, style, posture, and presentation while keeping your own natural features.
